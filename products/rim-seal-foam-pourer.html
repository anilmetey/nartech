E Rim Seal Foam Pourer:E Rim Seal Foam Pourer:

Rim Seal Foam Pourer is a specialized fire protection device designed specifically to protect the annular ring (rim seal area) of external floating-roof storage tanks, delivering expanded foam directly to the most vulnerable sealing zone where rim fires are most likely to occur.

NFPA 11 Compliance: Engineered in strict accordance with NFPA 11 standards to ensure proper air aspiration, reliable low-expansion foam generation, and targeted discharge performance directly onto the seal area.

UL / FM Approvals: Backed by trusted UL and FM certifications, guaranteeing top-tier manufacturing quality, operational precision, and maximum safety for critical tank rim fire protection.
